## Why this folder exists separately from `first_step/`

`first_step/` was built on the assumption that Remote Leverage was hiring you **in-house** as their Data Analyst — that you would be measuring their own funnel, and that the third interview would be with their internal hiring manager.

The slide shown in the group session says something different:

> **3 Part Interview:** 1. Group interview · 2. One on One Interview with Recruiters · 3. One on One Interview with **Hiring Manager/Client**
>
> *From start to finish, you can expect to get a job in 1–4 weeks if you pass.*

That word "Client" is the whole story. You are in the **placement pipeline**, not the in-house one. Remote Leverage is a broker: they charge the client a one-time flat fee, the client pays you directly afterwards, and a partner company handles payroll and compliance. Their product is you.

This changes who you are persuading and of what. It does not make `first_step/` useless — the SQL, the metrics vocabulary, the Tableau work and the question bank all become relevant again at stage three. But none of it is what stage two is decided on.

## The three things that decide stage two

Stage two is not a technical interview. Nobody will ask you to write SQL. A placement recruiter is answering one internal question: *can I put this person in front of a paying client without it blowing up?* That resolves into three checks.

**Can he actually work remotely from where he is?** You are in Haiti. Remote Leverage's own marketing says it sources from Latin America and the Philippines. Haiti is neither the region they advertise nor a place a US client assumes has reliable power and internet. This will come up, it will come up early, and vague reassurance will fail. Module 02 exists entirely for this.

**Is his English client-ready?** Their whole differentiator is English-speaking talent. Expect the call itself to be the test, and possibly a recorded assessment on top of it.

**Is he matchable to something currently open?** You told the group you would consider a data analyst role or a developer role. Handled well that widens the set of requisitions you fit. Handled badly it reads as "I'll take anything," which is how technical candidates get slotted into $900-a-month general admin work. Module 03 is the fix.

Everything else — your SQL, your Power BI, your Beam pipelines — is stage three material. Do not spend stage-two prep time on it.

## Standing work between now and stage three

You said you are working through Power BI and Tableau on YouTube. Keep doing it, but understand what it buys you: **it is stage-three insurance, not stage-two preparation.** The recruiter will not test it. The client will.

The one thing worth finishing early is a **published Tableau Public link**. It converts "I'd pick it up quickly" into a URL you can paste into a chat window mid-interview, and it is the only artefact that survives being passed from recruiter to client. `first_step/03_tableau_in_five_days.md` and the CSVs in `first_step/practice/csv/` are still the fastest path to one.
