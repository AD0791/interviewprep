## Three things this call is about

**Can he work from Haiti, reliably?** → volunteer the numbers, unprompted.
**Is his English client-ready?** → slow down. Pause instead of filling.
**What can I match him to?** → technical, senior. Open on title, not on level.

## The line that protects the rate

> **"I'm open on the title, not on the level. Analyst or developer — both technical, and I'd expect to be matched accordingly."**

And once, warmly:
> "Not looking for general VA or admin work — my background is technical."

## Money

**Deflect first:** *"What band are the technical requisitions you're placing at?"* — **then stop talking.**

If forced: target + reason, then hand back a question.
If below floor: *"That's below where I can go — but if something comes up nearer [target] I'd be very interested."*

**A range is heard as its bottom.** Never state a range starting at your floor.

## Ask these — the first two are not optional

1. **What roles are you matching me to, and what band?**
2. **Do you place candidates based in Haiti? Payment constraints?**
3. What's stage three — client directly? Technical?
4. Timeline, and any assessment or profile I should do now?
5. What separates placements that work from ones that don't?
6. Terms: payment method, hours, guarantee period, exclusivity?

---

## While talking

Slow down. Pause instead of "um."
Answer, then stop — do not keep going to fill silence.
Numbers, not adjectives.
No complaints about any past employer. Ever.
Write down every role and number they mention.

---

## After — same day

Thank-you note within a couple of hours. Reply to anything they send within the hour.
Write down: which requisitions, what band, what stage three looks like, what they promised next.
Then start building for whichever track they named.
